Built for Precision, Speed & Autonomy

Everything required to simulate mechanical systems in real-time, rendered with custom shaders and procedural sound design.

Standalone Single HTML

Three.js runtime, fonts, shaders, and geometry are compiled into one single file. Zero external requests, offline ready, and instantly shareable.

Calculated From First Principles

Parts are driven by explicit geometric equations — linkage circle intersections, rack and pinions, slider-cranks, and spring-damper dynamics.

Automated Guided Tour

When left alone, an intelligent camera director walks viewers through each subsystem, animating angles, annotations, and exploded views.

Procedural Audio Synthesizer

A Python-based sound effects framework models piston detonations, gear whines, and aerodynamic airflow in sync with timeline cues.

Live Telemetry HUD

Every gauge and readout computes active numbers directly from the physical model: stroke displacement, pressure bar, torque, and RPM.

Playwright 60 FPS Recorder

Deterministic frame-by-frame rendering engine guarantees zero dropped frames, outputting studio-mastered 60 fps videos directly with FFmpeg.

Build Any Custom Machine with MechViz

Point MechViz at any mechanism. It designs the 3D geometry in code, sets up the orbit lighting, hooks up the instrument HUD, and outputs the simulator.
